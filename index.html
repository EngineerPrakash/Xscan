Help

What can I open?

  • DICOM — a folder of slices, individual .dcm files, a multi-frame file, or a .zip of them (uncompressed or baseline-JPEG).
  • NIfTI — .nii or .nii.gz.
  • Plain PNG / JPG panoramic or bitewing X-ray images (viewed as a flat image).

3D view

  • Surface — solid rendering. Raise the Threshold to peel away soft tissue and show bone, then teeth.
  • X-ray — simulated radiograph (like a panoramic/ceph projection) you can rotate.
  • MIP — shows only the densest structure along each ray; good for implants and canals.
  • Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, Shift-drag or right-drag to pan. Crop cuts the volume open.

Slices

  • Click or drag in a slice to move the crosshair; the other views follow.
  • Use Slab thickness to average neighbouring slices into a thick, X-ray-like slice.
  • Use the Ruler tool to measure distances in millimetres.

Xscan is a viewer for personal use. It is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis or treatment planning.
